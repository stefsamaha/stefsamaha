# Stefanie Samaha

**Quantitative Research · Machine Learning · Applied Probability · Agentic AI Systems**

I build machine learning and data-driven systems for noisy, high-dimensional problems where evaluation matters: financial prediction, enterprise retrieval, statistical anomaly detection, and agentic browser automation.

My work sits at the intersection of **quantitative research, applied ML, and production AI engineering**. I care about models that are not only accurate, but also leakage-safe, robust under distribution shift, interpretable enough to debug, and useful under real-world constraints.

I am currently in **M1 (the first year) of the X-HEC MSc Data Science & AI** at **École Polytechnique and HEC Paris**, and an **incoming Quantitative Research Intern at Millennium**. I graduated from the **American University of Beirut** with a degree in **Computer & Communications Engineering** and a **4.0/4.0 GPA**.

Alongside my studies, I conduct **social science research under the supervision of Étienne Ollion and Julien Boelaert**, exploring the question: **Can AI replace human participants in surveys and experiments?**

Recent work includes AI/RAG systems at **Murex**, agentic browser automation at **Gaya.ai**, and independent quantitative research projects in stock prediction, alpha ranking, option pricing, and systematic strategies.

## Applied ML & AI Systems

I am especially interested in ML systems where the data is incomplete, adversarial, non-stationary, or operationally messy.

Recent work includes:

- Building leakage-safe stock prediction pipelines with point-in-time features, out-of-fold target encoding, time-series CV, and model ensembles
- Designing cross-sectional equity ranking systems evaluated with Spearman IC, turnover, drawdown, and out-of-sample degradation checks
- Pricing European and American options through risk-neutral valuation, Markov-chain lattices, and dynamic programming
- Deploying enterprise RAG pipelines over 100K+ documents using scalable embedding, chunking, deduplication, retrieval, and evaluation workflows
- Building agentic browser automation systems with live DOM extraction, reusable YAML/handler architecture, entity tracking, recovery logic, and safe handoff flows
- Framing low-rate DDoS detection as statistical anomaly detection over real-time traffic distributions with entropy features, Bayesian updating, and adaptive thresholds
